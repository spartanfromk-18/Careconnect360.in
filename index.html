The ₹500 fee confirms your booking slot, triggers our matching process, and covers coordinator time. It is fully refundable if we cannot place a suitable nurse within 4 hours of booking. The nurse's care fees are separate and settled directly with the nurse based on shift duration and service type.
Every nurse completes a 5-stage verification: (1) Government-issued ID, (2) Indian Nursing Council or State Nursing Council registration check, (3) criminal record clearance, (4) two professional references, and (5) a clinical skills assessment by our medical advisor. Verified nurses receive a CareConnect360 badge on their profile.
